Four places to start with

If it is your first time in Kerala, these give you the best mix of water, hills and coast.

Alleppey

Canals, coconut palms and houseboats that drift from morning to dusk.

Munnar

Rolling tea estates at 1,600 metres, cool air and mist before noon.

Kovalam

Crescent beaches, a lighthouse, and fresh seafood on the sand.

Periyar

Boat safaris and forest walks where elephants come down to the lake.

Things to do

  • Stay on a houseboat

    Sleep on the water, eat home-cooked fish curry on deck.

  • Walk a tea estate

    Join a morning trail and taste tea straight from the factory.

  • Watch Kathakali

    See the make-up, the mudras and the drums in a live evening show.

  • Try an Ayurvedic massage

    A traditional oil treatment by trained therapists.

  • Visit a spice garden

    Smell cardamom, pepper and clove where they grow.

  • Chase a waterfall

    Athirappilly Falls is best right after the monsoon.
